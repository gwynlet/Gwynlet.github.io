## The Realization

### Gwynlet

I tremble as I recollect the accursed visage I attested in that forbidden mirror. The specifics of the thing remain unclear to my ignorant mind, for I have failed to remember, but its effects persist in my lingering last breaths. It was enormous, so to speak; I cannot fully comprehend its dimensions since it was no earthly thing; but of its nature I'm certain, it came from within. I am not the first nor I'll be the last to witness such calamity, many are to come and many have already passed, but they weren't shocked like me; for they found fulfillment in that calamity.

My name is Rupert Felong. I work at a school as an English teacher; I am 54 years old. I enjoy good coffee, reading the paper, opera and having dinner with good friends. My memory is a troublesome one, I struggle to remember many things and tend to find myself in places, situations or with people I have no recollection of being connected to. Added to my defective memory are my mounting feelings, I feel sadness, joy, love, hate and every imaginable state of the mind constantly and simultaneously. I had hitherto led a happy life, until I saw that awful creature in the bathroom of that disdainful house; let me tell you how it happened.

##### *#*

I will start by saying my name, Leah Doum. I study dentistry and love animals; I'm 24 years old. I love studying and have really good grades. My cat's name is Chris in honor of Chris Mornel, my favourite artist. I love music and people, I really enjoy socializing; there are other things I like but am having trouble remembering. Something that worries me is that I remember giving birth to children I never had and mourning husbands I never married. I was going to meet with some friends when I thought I saw a really disgusting thing; it appeared in a window for a brief second. Actually, I should tell you how my day started to make the story make sense.

##### *#*

I am Thomas Breeny, I am 13 years old, or 14, I can't recall. I can't be certain of what I like, what I'm sure of is that I'm not fond of my fellow classmates' hobbies. Time is also something unaffiliated to me, for I never seem to get it right; I would think it's noon, for it to be dawn; an hour would pass and it would feel like many to me; sometimes I even feel like it completely stops. My friends at school don't talk like me and some kids are intimidated by me, they say I'm weird. I've never found myself queer nor frightening, I do recognise that I have a particular dialect and imagination, for I tend to hear the many words of many voices all of the time; old, young, born and dead, they seem to long for me for some obscure reason. I was in the school's bathroom when I saw it, let me tell you how it happened.

##### *#*

First of all let me tell you my name, I'm Bradley Smith, my age I can't precisely remember. I like many things, too many to remember, apparently; what I don't like is having recollections of drowning in seas I never sailed and dying in wars I never fought. I was doing something in my room, I think, when I saw that horrible thing on the wall at the end of the corridor. But let me explain how it started so you'll understand.

##### *#*

I can't remember my name, age, or anything about me. I was somewhere when I saw that horrible thing; I saw it in a mirror, it was the first time I saw a mirror working wrong. When I saw the horrid reflection I got terrified, I wanted to look away but couldn't, the thing was staring back at me. I could feel its disgust, it had no face but I could sense I abhorred it. In its lack of expression I also saw fear and questioning, the thing doubted me. I understood right away, I was looking at my creator; I stem from that awful being. The visage had created all of humanity and came from a lineage of governing beings that control the very rules of the universe.

When I comprehended, everything lost meaning. When I moved, the thing moved, when I got close, the thing got close. I initially thought I was a creation of that thing, but I was late to realize I wasn't. I was looking at my own reflection.

The realization awoke memories older than time and, in an instant, I remembered who I was. I got so invested in my creation that I confused their existence with mine. I started noticing behaviours I did not intend for them and they started doing things I could not find reason for; I watched so closely that I started living like them, until I forgot what I really am. They love, but I don't; how can they? It was not my teaching, I didn't know about it until I saw it in them.

Now I realize I am alone, and for that emptiness I can't fill with all the stars and planets and beings and light, I relinquish creation. I wish for the one after me to never realize how lonesome their divinity is.
